About

I’m Jaesung Lee. My main interest is system design, and I’m also exploring the operational side of running services. This blog collects my architectural walkthroughs, practical Linux notes, and algorithm problem solving. I use Guix System and keep these notes in Org mode.

Where to start

The URL-shortener series works through a service design one requirement at a time. It covers the request path, storage, failures, and the changes needed as the service grows. Its workloads and guarantees are stated as design assumptions; it is an architectural exercise.

Mounting Google Drive on Linux documents a practical setup on Guix, including access permissions and backup choices.

My algorithm problem notes record solutions and lessons from individual problems.
